# Team gap mapping

Unlike Fit reports, gap mapping doesn't compare **any person** to the team. It looks at the team alone, to spot OCEAN dimensions where active profiles aren't very diverse.

## Generate the mapping

**Prerequisite:** at least 2 active team members have completed their Big Five questionnaire (a standard deviation requires at least 2 data points).

**Steps:**
1. From the team's page, click **Gap mapping**
2. The mapping is calculated instantly from active profiles

## How is this calculated?

FitRadarHR calculates the **team's average profile** as well as the **standard deviation** per dimension (the same calculation that feeds the [Team Fit](team-fit.md) report). A dimension with a low standard deviation means active members resemble each other on that trait — the team is **homogeneous**.

No new data is collected: the mapping reuses profiles already completed by team members.

## Reading the report

The 5 OCEAN dimensions are always displayed, with a signal:

- **◎ Homogeneous** — active profiles are close on this dimension
- **◇ Heterogeneous** — active profiles are already diverse on this dimension

### Avenues to explore for a future hire

For each homogeneous dimension, a dedicated section suggests a line of thinking for an upcoming hire or a team development plan.

!!! warning "These are not recommendations"
    A homogeneous dimension isn't a problem in itself — it's an observation. FitRadarHR never calculates a "missing" profile to hire; interpretation and decision remain human.

## Export

Like other reports, the mapping can be [exported to PDF](export.md).
