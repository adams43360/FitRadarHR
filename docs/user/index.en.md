# FitRadarHR

**FitRadarHR** is a source-available tool that helps HR teams and managers assess the personality fit of a candidate or employee with a position or a team, based on the scientific **Big Five / OCEAN** model.

---

## Why FitRadarHR?

Recruitment and internal mobility too often rely on gut feeling. FitRadarHR brings a structured, transparent and ethical method:

- ✅ **Scientifically validated questionnaire** — the Big Five model (IPIP) is the world reference in personality psychology
- ✅ **No single decision score** — FitRadarHR informs, it never decides
- ✅ **GDPR by design** — explicit consent, right to erasure, traceability
- ✅ **Source-available code** — [Fair Source license (FSL-1.1-MIT)](about/license.md), self-hostable on your own infrastructure for internal use

---

## How does it work?

```
1. Create a position or a team
       ↓
2. Define the target Big Five profile
       ↓
3. Send a questionnaire to the person (secure link by email)
       ↓
4. The person answers (10–20 min)
       ↓
5. Review the fit report (radar chart per dimension)
```

---

## Who is it for?

| Profile | Use case |
|---|---|
| **HR manager** | Assess candidates for a position |
| **Manager** | Analyze how well an employee fits a team |
| **Solo user** | Manage candidates and teams independently (personal account) |

---

!!! info "Core principle"
    FitRadarHR **never** produces a binary recommendation (recommended / not recommended). Reports feed human judgment — the decision always stays in your hands.
