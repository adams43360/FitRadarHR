# Managing teams

A **team** in FitRadarHR groups employees whose Big Five profiles have been collected. **Team Fit** measures how well a new member would integrate into the existing collective dynamic.

## What you can do

- [Create a team](create.md) and assign it a manager
- [Add members](members.md) (employees with a completed Big Five profile)
- Generate a [Team Fit report](../reports/team-fit.md) for a candidate or a future member

## Concept: Team Fit

Team Fit doesn't compare a person to an abstract ideal, but to the **team's actual composition** — the distribution of existing profiles.

!!! info "Complementarity vs conformity"
    FitRadarHR measures compatibility, not conformity. A very extraverted team may benefit from a more reserved profile to balance the dynamic — it's up to the manager to interpret.
