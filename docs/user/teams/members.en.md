# Manage team members

## Adding a member

A member must be a **person who has completed their Big Five questionnaire**. Without a Big Five profile, the person cannot be included in the Team Fit calculation.

1. Open the team's page
2. Click **Add member**
3. Search for the person by name or email
4. Confirm the addition

## Removing a member

Click **Remove** next to the member. Membership history is kept (start and end dates) for traceability purposes.

## Membership history

FitRadarHR keeps the full history: a removed member still appears in reports generated during their membership period.

!!! info "GDPR"
    If a member exercises their right to erasure, their personal data (name, email) is anonymized. Associated reports are kept without a nominative link.
