# Create an account

FitRadarHR offers two types of accounts depending on your situation.

## Choosing your account type

On the home page, click **Create an account**. You'll land on a choice page:

### 🏢 Organization account (B2B)

Ideal if you are **HR or a manager at a company** and want to invite colleagues.

**Information requested:**

- Organization name
- Your first and last name
- Your professional email address
- Password

Upon creation, you get the **HR Manager** role and can invite Managers.

---

### 👤 Personal account (solo)

Ideal if you work **alone** (independent HR consultant, solo recruitment agency, etc.).

**Information requested:**

- Your first and last name
- Your email address
- Password

A personal space named *"[First name]'s space"* is created automatically. You have combined HR and Manager rights.

---

## Later login

Go to the home page and click **Log in**. Use the email and password chosen at sign-up.

!!! tip "Forgot your password?"
    Click **Forgot password** on the login page. A reset email will be sent to you.
